About Sip&Groove

Sip&Groove is urban music inspired by everyday life in Jakarta — the crowded mornings, endless meetings, coffee breaks, traffic, paydays, quiet nights, weekend escapes, and all the little dramas in between.

We turn those moments into songs.

Not because we have all the answers. Not to tell you how life should be lived. Just to share the mood, the stories, and the little things we all go through.

Jakarta is our backdrop, but the feelings are universal. The pressure, the excitement, the boredom, the small wins, the bad days, the “ya udah, jalanin aja” moments — chances are, you've been there too.

Sip&Groove is made with the help of AI, but the stories behind the music come from real moments, real observations, and everyday experiences happening around us.

So take what feels familiar. Find something good in it. And keep moving.

Life Goes On. Let's Groove Along.
